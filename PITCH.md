The one differentiator that beats all of them at once

None of the documented tools above combine all three of: (1) real code execution instead of LLM-estimated correctness, (2) an inspectable decision log showing exactly why difficulty/topic changed, and (3) persistent cross-session memory that reprioritizes what gets retested. Each competitor has at most one adjacent piece of this
vs. Pramp / Exponent
Exponent teaches interview frameworks before practice begins, addressing the gap where Pramp assumes you already know how to answer and just need live reps — but both are peer-matched or curriculum-based, not personalized to your specific resume+JD combination in real time. RoleReady's difference: the interview plan itself is generated per-candidate from your actual resume and target JD, not a fixed curriculum or a random peer's questions.

vs. Interviewing.io
Real engineers giving live feedback, $179–$300+ per session, $2,000 for a 3-session coaching package — the strength here is genuine human judgment, which no AI fully replicates. RoleReady's difference: not better human judgment, but availability and cost — unlimited sessions at a few cents each, plus code that's actually executed rather than judged by a (possibly distracted) volunteer engineer skimming your screen share.

vs. Final Round AI
The closest competitor architecturally. Per an independent review, it "is not deeply adaptive yet... reacts well, but doesn't strategize across the full interview arc," and its analytics are "descriptive, not predictive." RoleReady's difference: arc-level planning (a 5-stage plan built upfront, not just turn-by-turn reaction) plus a logged, inspectable decision trail for every adaptation — and confirmed real code execution vs. Final Round AI's coding feature being undocumented on whether it executes or estimates.

vs. Yoodli
Focused specifically on delivery — pace, filler words, structure — not content correctness — Yoodli tells you how you sound, not whether your answer was technically right. RoleReady's difference: doesn't touch delivery/speech coaching at all (real gap, be upfront), but evaluates content correctness on a rubric, including running your code — a different axis entirely, not really a competitor so much as a complementary tool.

vs. Revarta
Positions itself against "agreeable AI" like ChatGPT/Claude — "the honest, hiring-manager-calibrated feedback that agreeable AI can't" — focused on behavioral/STAR coaching specifically, voice-based. RoleReady's difference: covers both technical (with real code execution) and behavioral in one continuous dual-persona session, rather than being behavioral-only.

vs. MockIF
Leads for behavioral interviews with real-time adaptive pressure — behavioral-focused adaptivity. RoleReady's difference: adapts across both technical depth and behavioral rounds using the same underlying rubric-and-decision-log system, not just pressure/pacing on the behavioral side.

vs. Google Interview Warmup
Free, low-pressure, generic warm-up tool — not really a direct competitor, more of an on-ramp. RoleReady's difference: personalized to a specific resume+JD, with a real technical evaluation loop, rather than generic warm-up questions.
